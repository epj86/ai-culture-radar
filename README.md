# AI & Culture Intelligence Radar

**Independent applied AI research at the intersection of human behavior, culture, and emerging technology.**

**Research lead:** Errick Page  
**Status:** Public research project · foundational framework, no empirical findings yet  
**Focus:** AI adoption, cultural context, model evaluation, consumer trust, and business implications.

## Research question

How do emerging AI systems interpret cultural context, shape human behavior, and affect the ways people create, work, and make decisions?

## What this project does

This repository provides a transparent, repeatable framework for monitoring AI developments and conducting small applied research studies. Each edition separates **verified observations**, **interpretations**, and **hypotheses that require testing**.

This project is not a collection of automated headlines. It is intended to translate evidence into clear, decision-useful research.

## Research tracks

1. **AI adoption and behavior:** Who uses AI, for what purpose, and why?
2. **Cultural context and model evaluation:** How reliably do AI systems interpret context-dependent language and cultural references?
3. **Work and opportunity:** How are AI tools changing tasks, skills, and access?
4. **Creative ecosystems and business:** How do AI products affect creators, brands, and audiences?

## First study

**Study 001: Can AI models interpret cultural context without overgeneralizing?**

The initial study will compare model responses to a curated set of prompts containing contextual ambiguity, culture-specific references, and variation in audience. The study will examine interpretation accuracy, calibration, and handling of uncertainty. **No test results have been collected or published yet.**

Read the [research protocol](research/001-cultural-context-evaluation.md).

## Research standards

- Record source URLs, publication dates, access dates, and limitations.
- Distinguish primary evidence from commentary and market speculation.
- Predefine evaluation criteria before testing.
- Report failures, inconclusive findings, and counterexamples.
- Do not collect identifiable user responses or restricted materials without appropriate permission.
- Do not publish client-confidential or proprietary work.

## How to use

Begin with [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md), then use the research brief and source log templates for each new investigation. To propose a study or correction, open a GitHub Issue.
