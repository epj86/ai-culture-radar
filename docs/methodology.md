# Methodology & Editorial Standards

## Evidence classifications

- **Primary:** Official technical documentation, academic papers reporting original methods/results, public datasets, product release notes, direct tests.
- **Secondary:** Journalism, expert analysis, reviews, synthesis of primary research.
- **Interpretation:** Researcher's analysis grounded in cited evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** Testable idea with a stated method, not yet empirically validated.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Clear question and scope
- [ ] Methods specified before interpretation
- [ ] Sources recorded with links, dates, and reliability notes
- [ ] Facts and analysis separately labeled
- [ ] Competing explanations considered
- [ ] Limitations and uncertainty reported
- [ ] Any quantitative figures reproducible
- [ ] Confidentiality and licenses reviewed

## Confidence labels

- **High:** Converging strong evidence directly supports a narrow claim.
- **Moderate:** Credible evidence supports the claim, but important uncertainty remains.
- **Low:** Preliminary or indirect evidence; further verification required.

These labels are qualitative editorial judgments, not statistically calibrated probabilities.

## Corrections

Open an issue describing the disputed claim, supporting evidence, and proposed correction. Preserve an edit note in the relevant report.
